Chromatic Drift

14 Tracks • 2025 • Instrumental Funk/Disco

A kaleidoscopic concept album exploring spectrum-storytelling through 12 keys. Each track lives in a unique musical space, pulling references from CHIC, Prince, Parcels, Khruangbin, Daft Punk, Japanese City Pop, Egyptian Funk, and more. Journey from Nile Rodgers-style grooves to Arabian disco-funk, from French Riviera sunsets to clinical Vulfpeck precision.

Better Times 3000

12 Tracks • 2025 • Disco-Funk with Vocals

A warm, nostalgic journey through the golden era of 1970s and 1980s dance music. Inspired by CHIC's timeless elegance, Bee Gees' shimmering disco, and Daft Punk's modern polish. Features Nile Rodgers-style guitar chanks, Bernard Edwards-influenced basslines, and warm female backing vocals throughout. Each track feels like stepping onto a disco floor under silver lights—fast, funky, glamorous, and full of movement.

About the Artist

Stoch is an AI-driven music project that bridges the gap between classic funk & disco and futuristic sound design. Each track is crafted with attention to the golden era's groove philosophy while embracing modern production techniques and AI-assisted composition.

Drawing inspiration from legends like Nile Rodgers, Bernard Edwards, Prince, and Daft Punk, as well as contemporary innovators like Parcels, Khruangbin, and Vulfpeck, Stoch creates music that feels both timeless and forward-thinking.
